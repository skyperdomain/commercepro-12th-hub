1. Accounting for Partnership Firms

• Goodwill (Super Profit): $\text{Super Profit} = \text{Average Profit} - \text{Normal Profit}$. $\text{Goodwill} = \text{Super Profit} \times \text{Years Purchase}$.

• Sacrificing Ratio: $\text{Old Share} - \text{New Share}$. Used for goodwill distribution among existing partners upon admission.

Case Study Decoding Strategy

  1. Read the final question first: Directly check what management term is asked before reading the story.
  2. Identify Trigger Keywords: Look for specific words like "span of control", "delegation", or "demarketing".
  3. Quote specific lines: In CBSE evaluation, quoting context lines from the case yields full marks.

Economics Master Guide

National Income formulas, Multiplier calculations, and Indian Economic Development key facts.

National Income Formulas

  • Expenditure Method: $GDP_{MP} = C + I + G + (X - M)$.
  • Income Method: $NDP_{FC} = \text{COE} + \text{Operating Surplus} + \text{Mixed Income}$.
  • Investment Multiplier ($k$): $k = \frac{1}{1 - MPC} = \frac{1}{MPS}$.

Accountancy • 6 Marks Question CBSE Board 2024

Q: Differentiate between Dissolution of Partnership and Dissolution of Partnership Firm.

Answer Key: Dissolution of partnership refers to a change in the economic relation between partners (business continues), whereas dissolution of firm refers to the complete closure of business operations, realization of assets, and settlement of liabilities.

Business Studies • 4 Marks Question CBSE Board 2023

Q: Explain any four functions performed by a Financial Manager.

Answer Key: 1. Estimation of Capital Requirements. 2. Determination of Capital Composition. 3. Choice of Sources of Funds. 4. Investment of Funds & Management of Working Capital.
