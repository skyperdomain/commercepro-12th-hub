1. Goodwill Valuation Methods

  • Average Profit Method: Goodwill = Average Profit $\times$ Number of Years' Purchase.
  • Super Profit Method: Super Profit = Average Profit - Normal Profit. Goodwill = Super Profit $\times$ Number of Years' Purchase.
  • Capitalisation Method: Capitalised Value of Business minus Net Assets (Capital Employed).

3. Cash Flow Statement (AS-3) Quick Guidelines

Cash flows must be categorized into three activities: Operating Activities (principal revenue-generating activities, adjusted for non-cash items like depreciation and working capital changes), Investing Activities (purchase/sale of fixed assets and investments), and Financing Activities (issue of shares/debentures, loan repayment, and dividend paid).

Macroeconomics Formulas

  • Value Added Method: $GVA_{MP} = \text{Value of Output} - \text{Intermediate Consumption}$.
  • Income Method: $NDP_{FC} = \text{COE} + \text{Operating Surplus} + \text{Mixed Income}$.
  • Investment Multiplier ($k$): $k = \frac{1}{1 - MPC} = \frac{1}{MPS}$.
