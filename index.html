Chapter-Wise Formula & Rules

Partnership Goodwill Methods:

• Average Profit Method: Goodwill = Avg Profit × No. of Years Purchase.

• Super Profit Method: Super Profit = Avg Profit - Normal Profit. (Normal Profit = Capital Employed × NRR / 100).

Issue & Forfeiture of Shares Journal Entries:

Most Important Board Questions (PYQs)

Q1 (6 Marks):

What adjustments are required in partners' capital accounts upon the admission of a new partner?

Answer Key: Calculation of new profit sharing & sacrificing ratio, valuation and treatment of goodwill, revaluation of assets and reassessment of liabilities, distribution of accumulated profits/reserves, and capital adjustment.
